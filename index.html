POLICY WIN

No Bio dropped 73% YoY in April

April 2025 had 145 No-Bio events; April 2026 has just 39. Largest single-month YoY drop across any category — the strongest signal that strict policy enforcement is working.

Tardiness minutes down 28%

EFFICIENCY

STRUCTURAL

62% quarterly decline

Total violations Jan→Apr 2026: 383 → 273 → 188 → 147. Sustained downward trend, not seasonal volatility.
